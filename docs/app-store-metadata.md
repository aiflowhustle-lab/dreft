## Description

Dreft is a native SwiftUI workspace for thinkers, writers, and visual planners who want Obsidian-style power without giving up their data.

**Free to browse**
Open and read your notes and canvases, explore the graph view, and export your work — no subscription required.

**Dreft Pro — write everywhere**
Subscribe to create and edit notes, canvases, folders, and vaults on Mac and iPad. One subscription covers both platforms.

**Write in markdown**
Linked notes with wikilinks, bookmarks, and a fast file explorer sidebar.

**Think on an infinite canvas**
Note and image cards on a zoomable canvas. Connect ideas with styled lines, labels, and arrows.

**See the big picture**
Graph view shows how your notes connect.

**Your vault, your device**
Everything is stored locally in folders you control. No Dreft account. No cloud required.

**Built for Mac and iPad**
Custom chrome on Mac with split panes and keyboard shortcuts. Floating sidebar and touch toolbars on iPad.

**Export anytime**
Export canvases as PNG and notes as PDF — always free.

Dreft respects your privacy: no tracking, no data collection, no ads. Subscriptions are processed by Apple.

---

**Dreft Pro subscriptions (auto-renewable):**
• Yearly — $59.99/year with 3-day free trial for eligible new subscribers
• Monthly — $7.99/month

Payment is charged to your Apple ID. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the period. Manage subscriptions in App Store Settings.
