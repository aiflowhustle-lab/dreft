# Dreft Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** July 28, 2026

Dreft is a local-first notes and infinite-canvas workspace for Mac and iPad. Your privacy is built into how the app works: your notes and canvases stay on your device, in folders you choose.

---

## The short version

- **No accounts.** Dreft does not require sign-in.
- **No analytics, ads, or tracking.** We do not collect usage data or sell personal information.
- **Your vault stays local.** Notes, canvases, and bookmarks live in folders on your device.
- **Optional subscriptions** are processed entirely by Apple. We never see your payment card or billing details.
- **No Dreft servers** receive your writing or vault content.

---

## What Dreft stores, and where

Everything you create in Dreft — markdown notes, canvases, links between ideas, bookmarks, and app preferences — is saved **locally** in your vault (a folder on your device that you choose). You can move, back up, or delete these files at any time using Finder or the Files app.

Deleting the app or your vault folders removes this data. We cannot access, recover, or delete it for you, because it never leaves your device under our control.

### App preferences and subscription status (on your device only)

To run the app, Dreft also stores small amounts of data locally on your device, such as:

- Theme and layout preferences (for example sidebar width, open tabs)
- Security-scoped bookmarks so Dreft can reopen vault folders you select
- Onboarding completion status
- **Subscription access state** — whether you have Dreft Pro, are in a free trial, or are using read-only access after a subscription ends
- Whether you installed Dreft before version 1.2.0 (grandfathered full access)

This information is kept in your device’s local storage (UserDefaults and the Keychain) and is **not** sent to AI Flow Hustle or any third-party analytics service.

## Dreft Pro and In-App Purchases

Dreft offers an optional **Dreft Pro** subscription (yearly or monthly) through Apple’s In-App Purchase system.

**What Apple handles**

- Payment processing and billing
- Subscription renewal, cancellation, and refunds (under Apple’s policies)
- Family Sharing eligibility (if enabled for the product in App Store Connect)

**What we receive**

- We do **not** receive your name, email, payment method, or billing address from Apple.
- Dreft uses Apple **StoreKit** on your device to check whether your Apple ID has an active subscription or trial. That verification happens between your device and Apple’s servers.
- We do not operate subscription servers and we do not build a profile of your purchases outside what is stored locally on your device for access control.

**Restore Purchases** uses Apple’s standard restore flow so you can regain Pro access on a new device signed in with the same Apple ID.

For subscription terms, pricing, trials, and cancellation, see our [Terms of Service](https://lavish-birthday-3cc.notion.site/Dreft-Terms-of-Service-3ab2796a2453808d905fdf00abb30809).

## Photos and files

Dreft only accesses photos or files when **you explicitly choose one** — for example, when adding an image card using the Photos picker on iPad or the file picker on Mac. Dreft does not scan your photo library in the background. Selected images are stored in your vault alongside your notes. No photo data is transmitted to us.

---

## Network use

The core app works **fully offline**, including reading and editing your vault (when you have Pro access).

Dreft may use the network in these limited cases:

| When | Why |
|------|-----|
| You tap a link in the app | Opens a webpage in your browser (Help, Privacy Policy, Terms of Use) |
| You view or purchase Dreft Pro | StoreKit communicates with Apple to load products and verify subscription status |
| You tap **Restore Purchases** | Apple verifies prior purchases for your Apple ID |

Dreft does not “phone home,” run analytics, or send your vault content to our servers in the background — because we have no servers that receive it.

---

## What Dreft does not collect

- No user accounts or login to Dreft
- No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
- No advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking
- No camera access
- No location data
- No push notifications
- No sale or sharing of personal data — we do not receive your vault content or payment details

Dreft’s Apple privacy manifest declares **no tracking** and **no collected data types** reported to Apple’s privacy nutrition labels for data sent off-device by us. Subscription transactions are handled under Apple’s In-App Purchase privacy practices.

---

## Children’s privacy

Dreft does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Subscription purchases require an Apple ID subject to Apple’s age and parental controls. Because we do not operate accounts or receive vault content, there is no children’s data held by us.

---

## Your rights

Your notes and canvases are under **your direct control** on your device. Since we do not operate a cloud service or user accounts for your content, we do not hold copies of your vault that you would need to request access to or delete from us.

For subscription and billing questions, use Apple’s subscription management tools or contact us at the email below.

---

## Changes to this policy

If we change how Dreft handles data — for example, adding optional sync — we will update this policy and the “Last updated” date before that version is released. Material changes to subscription-related practices will be reflected here and in our Terms of Use.
